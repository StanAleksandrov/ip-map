Imaginary example: Brightmoor Robotics, its Workbench project, the people, the meetings and all 20 ideas are invented to show the method. 20 ideas in 5 clusters, from the recorded patent sessions of February – May 2026.
How to read the cardsIdea titles are in USPTO style; click any idea to open its abstract and the notes from the transcription in a pop-up window. Each card shows N = novelty / non-obviousness, C = completeness for filing, R = readiness, Impact = business impact, that is, competitive advantage for Workbench. Only the first idea under each cluster and idea 17, the first item of the committee deck, are filled in completely; for the other ideas the card shows the evaluation and the status, and the abstract, notes, people and sources are left empty.
